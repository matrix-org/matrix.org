## Dept of Servers 🏢

### Zendrite ([website](https://zendrite.pat-s.me))

[pat-s](https://matrix.to/#/@pat-s:matrix.devxy.io) announces

> [Zendrite](https://zendrite.pat-s.me) is a Matrix homeserver written in Go, forked from Dendrite earlier this year.
> v3.4.0 is out, and its headline feature is **active-passive high availability**.
>
> **High availability (opt-in).**
> Run several Zendrite candidates against shared PostgreSQL, NATS JetStream and S3-compatible media storage; a Kubernetes Lease elects one to serve while the others wait as standbys.
> A durable ownership generation fences every boundary (database, broker, media, outbound federation and request admission), so a paused or partitioned old leader cannot write after its successor takes over.
> Rolling upgrades between compatible builds hand over without failed client requests (search briefly answers 503 while the new leader rebuilds its index), and an upgrade contract refuses builds that would need an offline migration.
>
> HA changes are qualified in CI by a fault-injection harness that runs against real PostgreSQL, a three-node JetStream cluster, a kube-apiserver and a federating peer.
> It kills, freezes and partitions leaders, fails over PostgreSQL, loses brokers, and checks that no acknowledged write, to-device message or receipt goes missing.
>
> **Federation and sync.**
>
> - MSC4311 and knocking over federation.
> - Bundled aggregations for threads, edits and references on every endpoint that should carry them.
> - Per-thread unread counts (MSC3773).
> - Large rooms no longer stall sync: a state update in a room of 50,000 members now takes seconds instead of minutes.
>
> **Reliability.**
> Dozens of fixes found while qualifying HA also benefit single-server setups:
>
> - Federation transactions are retried instead of silently dropping receipts or to-device messages when the broker cannot take them.
> - A silent NATS broker is detected in about 30 seconds.
> - Consumers retry transient failures instead of losing work.
> - New metrics make a stalled consumer or a struggling broker visible.
>
> Upgrade notes, the full changelog and the HA guide are in the [docs](https://zendrite.pat-s.me/administration/high-availability/) and the [release](https://codefloe.com/pat-s/zendrite/releases).
> Chat with us in [#dendrite:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#dendrite:matrix.org).

### Synapse ([website](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/))

Synapse is a Matrix homeserver implementation developed by Element.

[madlittlemods (Eric Eastwood)](https://matrix.to/#/@madlittlemods:matrix.org) reports

> This week we have a new release, [Synapse 1.162.0](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/blob/release-v1.162/CHANGES.md#synapse-11620-2026-09-29). To highlight:
>
>  - The default room version is now room version 12 ([#20130](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20130), thanks to [@jason-famedly](https://github.com/jason-famedly) from Famedly).
>  - As a reminder, we've been driving forward Matrix spec compliance by filling in a bunch of tiny gaps in behavior between Synapse and the Matrix spec. This release includes even more fixes ([#20115](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20115), [#20154](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20154), [#20181](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20181), [#20200](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20200)). And we're at the point that we will be advertising support for more Matrix spec versions in the next release ✨.
>  - Also as part of the Matrix spec compliance march, we fixed the flawed [MSC4311](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4311) implementation originally introduced in [Synapse v1.136.0](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/blob/release-v1.162/CHANGES.md#synapse-11360-2025-08-12) ([#19723](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/19723)). This is a fix around stripped state for invites/knocks: client-side APIs like `/sync` use [stripped state events](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.18/client-server-api/#stripped-state) and we now send full PDUs on the federation-side. This also fixes the incompatibility that [Continuwuity v26.6.1+](https://forgejo.ellis.link/continuwuation/continuwuity/releases/tag/v26.6.1) folks have been experiencing since the stripped state format is strictly validated there. To give the ecosystem time to adapt, Synapse will only apply strict validation to incoming invites and knocks after 2027-06-01.
>  - `/sync` no longer drops state events in [MSC4222](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4222)'s `state_after` when the `since` token falls inside a persisted batch of events ([#20171](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20171)).
>  - `/relations` got a speed boost in large rooms (10-100x purportedly) which means opening the thread panel should be much quicker ([#20182](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20182)).
>  - We got some help with one of the pesky Complement test flakes to ensure the `/room_summary` endpoint returns fresh and accurate `join_rules` ([#20205](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/pull/20205), thanks to [@jason-famedly](https://github.com/jason-famedly) from Famedly)
>
> Thank you to all our contributors for helping to make Synapse the best it can be. As always, feel free to stop by [#synapse:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#synapse:matrix.org) to join in on the discussion and if you encounter a bug make sure to [report it](https://github.com/element-hq/synapse/issues/new/choose).

> * Fixed a blank screen at launch when the homeserver expires the sliding sync session, and fixed a leak of threaded timelines.
>

The sign out and signed-out screens have been redesigned, now displaying account info and scrolling properly on small screens. Automatic back pagination and unread indicators are now enabled by default, and you can choose to display only unread notifications. Search history got buttons to remove a single item or clear the whole history. As usual, plenty of bug fixes and dependency upgrades landed too, including a crash fix in voice message playback.
