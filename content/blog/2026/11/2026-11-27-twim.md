## Dept of Servers 🏢

### Zendrite ([website](https://zendrite.pat-s.me))

[pat-s](https://matrix.to/#/@pat-s:matrix.devxy.io) announces

> [Zendrite](https://codefloe.com/pat-s/zendrite), the Dendrite fork written in Go, released v3.10.0.
> This release adds room retention policies, more MatrixRTC support and new moderation tools.
>
> **Retention and moderation**
>
> - [MSC1763](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/1763) room retention policies: events past a room's retention policy are no longer served in sync, over federation or in `/notifications`, and are purged from the database.
> - [MSC4194](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4194) lets moderators redact a user's events in a room, and [MSC4293](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4293) redacts them as part of a kick or ban.
> - Refused sends and membership changes now name the reason ([MSC3848](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3848)).
>
> **MatrixRTC**
>
> - Zendrite now issues LiveKit tokens itself, removes departed members from LiveKit SFUs and delegates delayed leaves ([MSC4195](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4195), MatrixRTC with a LiveKit backend).
> - The stable `rtc/transports` endpoint from [MSC4143](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4143), which tells clients which RTC transports the server offers.
> - Push rules ring for MatrixRTC call notifications ([MSC4075](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4075)).
>
> **Application services and clients**
>
> - Application services receive to-device events ([MSC4203](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4203)), answer key queries for their users ([MSC3983](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3983), [MSC3984](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3984)) and can serve parts of the APIs ([MSC4512](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4512)).
> - Clients can replace a whole profile ([MSC4437](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4437)), query several profiles at once ([MSC4536](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4536)) and delete account data ([MSC3391](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3391)).
> - Push rules can match on related events ([MSC3664](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3664)).
> - Operators can opt in to refusing access tokens in query strings ([MSC4127](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4127)) and to limiting key queries to users who share a room ([MSC4263](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4263)).
>
> **Federation and fixes**
>
> - Ambiguous federation JSON is rejected ([MSC4458](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4458)), well-known redirects are followed only over https ([MSC4402](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4402)) and EDUs are limited to 64 KiB ([MSC4514](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4514)).
> - Room version 12 invites whose stripped state lacks `m.room.create` are rejected.
> - Sliding sync keeps to-device messages past its limit and sends typing and receipts only for joined rooms.
> - `/register` now requires the UIA session the server issued.
> - Guests can accept federated invites.
> - The docs moved to [docs.zendrite.xyz](https://docs.zendrite.xyz).
>
> Release notes: <https://codefloe.com/pat-s/zendrite/releases/tag/v3.10.0>  
> Chat: [#matrix:zendrite.xyz](https://matrix.to/#/#matrix:zendrite.xyz)

### Element X iOS ([website](https://github.com/element-hq/element-x-ios))

A total rewrite of Element iOS using the Matrix Rust SDK underneath and targeting devices running iOS 17+.

[Mauro](https://matrix.to/#/@mauro.romito:element.io) announces

> Hi everyone! Here's what the Element X iOS team has been up to this week:
> 
> #### ✨ New and improved
>
> - **Custom emoji**: you can now react with a room's own emoji from the reaction picker and search them by shortcode. Room details also has a new "Emoji and stickers" screen where you can browse the room's packs.
> - **Reaction celebrations**: the particle effects we were trying out are now on for everyone, with gentler floating hearts. They stay off when Reduce Motion is on.
> - **iPhone Duo**: the app now uses the standard tab bar, which fits the Duo much better.
> 
> #### 🚧 In progress
>
> - **Multi-account support**: the verification and sign-out screens now show which account they're for, and a verification request can appear whichever account you're using. If an account is signed out from another device, a screen now tells you which one.
> - **Stickers**: sending stickers from a room's sticker packs is coming next.
> - **Sharing**: we're redesigning the share sheet so you can pick up to five chats and send to all of them at once.
> 
> #### 🐛 Fixes
>
> - Files shared into the app from apps like Files now keep their original name.
> - Fixed a crash when sharing to the app while a picture was open full screen.
> - The keyboard no longer stays on top of a call when you go from picture-in-picture to full screen.
> - Custom emoji and image reactions now follow the "hide media in the timeline" setting.
> 
> #### 💜 Community
>
> - Thanks to @wykeenjenga for letting you swipe away the media preview while it's still loading.

### Sennit ([website](https://github.com/bostrot/sennit))

[Eric](https://matrix.to/#/@bostrot:matrix.aachen.dev) announces

> I'm building Sennit, a Matrix client for people who use a lot of bridges. Point it at your homeserver and your mautrix bridges, and the same person on WhatsApp, Signal and Telegram becomes one conversation, with a small badge on each message showing which network it came through. Bridge logins, QR codes included, run inside the app through the bridgev2 provisioning API, so there's no bot chat to drive.
> 
> This week:
> 
> - **OAuth / next-gen sign-in.** Servers that only offer OIDC, like Continuwuity behind an identity provider, now open their own login page and hand you back to Sennit. Thanks to the person on r/matrixdotorg who reported it.
> - **Sync between devices.** Merged chats and settings follow you to your other devices, encrypted with your key backup, so the homeserver only stores ciphertext.
> - **Sennit Web.** Your phone serves the app to a paired browser on your network, end-to-end encrypted, with nothing hosted.
> - Hindi and Arabic (right to left) joined the other six languages.
> 
> It runs on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux and is AGPL-3.0-only. Store builds have an optional one-time purchase for the AI features; a build from source has everything. It's still beta and rough in places, so feedback is very welcome in [#sennit:matrix.aachen.dev](https://matrix.to/#/#sennit:matrix.aachen.dev).
> 
> [GitHub](https://github.com/bostrot/sennit)
