Hostsharing eG is a German cooperative that operates its own servers in data centres in Germany.
The cooperative offers its members comprehensive, one-stop IT solutions.
Members can sign up for a customised, managed Matrix solution.
The offer includes storage replication to standby servers and remote backup.
The service is available in Germany and the European Union.
