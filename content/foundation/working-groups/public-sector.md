## Membership Process

Formal members of the working group need to be employed by a public sector organisations (or majority public sector owned organisation) to avoid any conflict of interest and create a safe sharing space.

Formal membership in the working group expires when a member is not employed by a public sector organisation any more.
Members are obliged to disclose the information that they are leaving the public sector without delay.

## Matrix Rooms

There is intentinally no restriction for participating in the activities and discussions via the [public Matrix room of the working group](https://matrix.to/#/#public-sector-wg:matrix.org) and the working group highly appriciates any contributions and ideas from community members or anyone interested in public sector IT.

Next to the public room, there's an additional, [invite-only public-sector-internal Matrix room]().
This second Matrix room is access-restricted to formal members of the working group.

We kindly ask vendors and private individuals to participate via the public Matrix room of the working group.

## Meetings

Meetings of the working group might be open to everyone, or restricted to formal members of the working group, depending on the meeting agenda.
